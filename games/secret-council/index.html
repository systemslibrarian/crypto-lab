      Why this is cryptography

      A secret you can lose a share of.
      Without losing the secret.

      Shamir secret sharing turns a secret into mathematical shares. Any group meeting the threshold can reconstruct it. Fewer shares reveal no information about its value when the sharing is correctly randomized.

      This can help protect a backup encryption key: some copies of shares can be lost or stolen without losing recovery or revealing the key. Your threshold and where you keep the shares determine which losses you can tolerate.
