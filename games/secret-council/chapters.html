    The idea behind the game

    Trust, shared.
    Power, balanced.

    A share is a mathematical point. Enough distinct points recover the secret. A smaller collection leaves every possible secret open.

    01

    A share is not a slice.

    One share does not reveal one piece of the message. With correctly randomized Shamir sharing, fewer than the threshold reveal no information about the secret.

    02

    Higher is a tradeoff.

    A larger threshold makes a larger coalition necessary to recover the secret. It also means fewer keepers can be absent. A council needs to plan for both.

    03

    Different people. Shared risks.

    Two keepers in the same building may disappear in the same outage. The later chapters ask you to distribute shares across independent locations.

    What this game demonstrates

    Real Shamir interpolation over the small field F₁₇; fictional keepers, thefts, and outages. The vault is a game indicator, not encrypted storage. This page holds the secret and all shares in one browser—use only its generated examples. Basic Shamir sharing does not authenticate shares, prevent copying, or erase a recovered secret. Trials are alternate scenarios, not a sequence of accumulating thefts.
