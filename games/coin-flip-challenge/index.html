Why XOR?

If both secret bits are independent and uniform, XOR is uniform even when one player tries to favor Heads. The commitment must be hiding and binding, and the protocol still needs an abort policy.

Four verdicts: Fair means verified openings and no premature leak. Forged means the reveal doesn't match the saved hash. Aborted means someone withholds a reveal. Leaky means the valid seal let someone recover the hidden bit early.

Inspect the full rules

The page computes SHA-256 over an unambiguous JSON array: ['crypto-lab-game-v1', label, bit, nonce]. The nonce is 16 bytes from Web Crypto. A bit may be 0 or 1; both parties' verified bits are XORed to make the outcome.

An opponent who aborts after seeing your reveal can deny the result. A commitment alone cannot guarantee completion, authentication, or enforce a deadline. This is a local one-browser simulation of remote players, not a secure live wagering service.
