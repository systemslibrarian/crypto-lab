# Learning paths

`index.html` defines `LEARNING_PATHS`: IDs, labels, descriptions, ordered steps, and optional side trips. Both navigation copies derive from this array; the README table is generated by `tools/readme-sync.js`.

The menu builds `.path-card` disclosures. Its step links clear category, search, and curated-set filters and scroll to the matching catalog card.

The homepage builds `.path-band-card` entries containing native `details` / `summary` disclosures. Clicking a summary, or using Enter or Space while it has focus, reveals the ordered lab list. Those links open the labs themselves. Descriptions, step lists, and side trips reuse the menu's generated content; level ranges derive from the referenced cards. “New here? Start here” expands the first path, scrolls to it, and focuses its summary.

Course-linked catalog cards live in `.card-cell` wrappers. The card stays in normal layout and fills the wrapper; only its sibling `.module-links` footer is absolutely positioned. Making the card absolute collapses the wrapper and makes cards overlap section headings.

Homepage path summaries share measured heading, metadata, and total heights, recalculated after fonts load and on resize. This aligns the collapsed cards and their controls without clipping text. Expanded lists grow independently.

Lattice Builder stays in the Post-Quantum section as a learning game. Its `data-level="beginner"` describes the opening puzzle; the visible “Beginner entry · Advanced exploration” line distinguishes its optional deeper modes. The editorial judgement lives in `tools/lab-levels.json`, so `level-sync` preserves it. `data-format="learning-game"` styles its format badge and play action without changing topic filters or algorithm evidence.

The Post-Quantum path places Lattice Builder and Lattice Gentle between PQ Families and Kyber Vault: orientation, geometric intuition, guided mathematics, then key establishment. The Lattice Gentle worksheet offers the puzzle as optional preparation outside its existing class-time estimate. The README, teaching pages and knowledge map are regenerated from these sources.

The Applied Cryptography path carries an optional `guide` link, rendered in both menu and homepage disclosures and generated into the README. Its `learn/applied-cryptography/` companion resolves authored chapter links against `knowledge-map/catalog.json`; run `node learn/applied-cryptography/build.mjs` after changing its data or linked catalog URLs. The guide has 25 chapter connections, explicit coverage boundaries and optional local-only exploration progress.

The Introduction to Modern Cryptography path uses the same `guide` mechanism and has its own direct menu link at `learn/modern-cryptography/`. Its 15 chapters follow the Katz–Lindell third-edition contents, with the revised-edition naming difference documented on the page. Chapter data adds original reading pointers, reflection questions and per-exhibit explanations/coverage labels. Run `node learn/modern-cryptography/build.mjs` to generate the complete no-JavaScript HTML; `--check` detects drift. Both guides link to each other.

Both book guides start with a compact book title and author/edition line, followed immediately by “Find your chapter,” its filters and the chapter connections. The introductory experiment cards have been removed. Coverage-label explanations, independent-companion notes and sources follow the chapters, while each chapter retains its own coverage boundary. `learn/shared/reading-path.css` supplies the shared compact layout, including a search row and paired filters on phones, without changing the protocol workshop layout.

The new path’s search treats `chapter 1` as an exact chapter-number query, so it does not also match chapters 10–15. Book-area and chapter-coverage filters compose with the search. Progress uses `crypto-lab-modern-cryptography-progress-v1`, distinct from the existing Schneier key; only valid chapter IDs are loaded, and malformed or denied storage leaves the page usable. Checkmarks record exploration, not mastery. The static page retains all links and disables progress controls until JavaScript initializes.

After changing a hub path, regenerate `README.md` with `node tools/readme-sync.js` and the knowledge-map catalog with `node tools/knowledge-map-sync.js`. The book companions are navigation/learning resources, so they do not change the standalone exhibit count.

The timestamping and fair-exchange workshops are hub-owned learning pages under `learn/`, not separate repositories or standalone catalog cards. Their number is not added to the standalone exhibit count. They are discoverable through the path and its chapter links.
