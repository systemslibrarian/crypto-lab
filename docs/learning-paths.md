# Learning paths

`index.html` defines `LEARNING_PATHS`: IDs, labels, descriptions, ordered steps, optional side trips, and an optional `home` flag. Both navigation surfaces derive from this array; the README table is generated by `tools/readme-sync.js`.

The menu builds `.path-card` disclosures. Its step links clear category, search, and curated-set filters and scroll to the matching catalog card.

The homepage skips records with `home: false`: the Applied Cryptography, Introduction to Modern Cryptography, Boneh–Shoup and Understanding Cryptography companions. They remain in the menu, the README table, the knowledge map and their direct reading-guide URLs. All other paths default to appearing on the homepage.

The homepage builds `.path-band-card` entries containing native `details` / `summary` disclosures. Clicking a summary, or using Enter or Space while it has focus, reveals the ordered lab list. Those links open the labs themselves. Descriptions, step lists, and side trips reuse the menu's generated content; level ranges derive from the referenced cards. “New here? Start here” expands the first path, scrolls to it, and focuses its summary.

Course-linked catalog cards live in `.card-cell` wrappers. The card stays in normal layout and fills the wrapper; only its sibling `.module-links` footer is absolutely positioned. Making the card absolute collapses the wrapper and makes cards overlap section headings.

Homepage path summaries share measured heading, metadata, and total heights, recalculated after fonts load and on resize. This aligns the collapsed cards and their controls without clipping text. Expanded lists grow independently.

Lattice Builder stays in the Post-Quantum section as a learning game. Its `data-level="beginner"` describes the opening puzzle; the visible “Beginner entry · Advanced exploration” line distinguishes its optional deeper modes. The editorial judgement lives in `tools/lab-levels.json`, so `level-sync` preserves it. `data-format="learning-game"` styles its format badge and play action without changing topic filters or algorithm evidence.

The Post-Quantum path places Lattice Builder and Lattice Gentle between PQ Families and Kyber Vault: orientation, geometric intuition, guided mathematics, then key establishment. The Lattice Gentle worksheet offers the puzzle as optional preparation outside its existing class-time estimate. The README, teaching pages and knowledge map are regenerated from these sources.

The Applied Cryptography path carries an optional `guide` link, rendered in its menu disclosure and generated into the README; homepage disclosures render the same link only for paths shown there. Its `learn/applied-cryptography/` companion resolves authored chapter links against `knowledge-map/catalog.json`; run `node learn/applied-cryptography/build.mjs` after changing its data or linked catalog URLs. The guide has 25 chapter connections, explicit coverage boundaries and optional local-only exploration progress.

The Introduction to Modern Cryptography path uses the same `guide` mechanism and has its own direct menu link at `learn/modern-cryptography/`. Its 15 chapters follow the Katz–Lindell third edition (2021), with the revised edition’s (2025) chapter outline cross-checked and its Chapter 14 naming difference shown on the card. Each chapter displays the printed book title, an original learning focus, targeted section pointers, a prediction before the lab links, and a try–explain activity. Per-exhibit notes and boundaries state the selected connection; visible labels say “Direct connection,” “Modern counterpart” and “Introductory bridge.” Run `node learn/modern-cryptography/build.mjs` to generate the complete no-JavaScript HTML; `--check` detects drift. Both guides link to each other.

Both book guides start with a compact book title and author/edition line, followed immediately by “Find your chapter,” its filters and the chapter connections. The introductory experiment cards have been removed. The modern guide adds a compact independent-companion line with an anchor to its edition and sources. Label explanations, full independence and edition notes, and sources follow the chapters, while each chapter retains its own coverage boundary. `learn/shared/reading-path.css` supplies the shared compact layout, including a search row and paired filters on phones, without changing the protocol workshop layout.

The new path’s search treats `chapter 1` as an exact chapter-number query, so it does not also match chapters 10–15. Book-area and chapter-coverage filters compose with the search. Progress uses `crypto-lab-modern-cryptography-progress-v1`, distinct from the existing Schneier key; only valid chapter IDs are loaded, and malformed or denied storage leaves the page usable. Checkmarks record exploration, not mastery. The static page retains all links and disables progress controls until JavaScript initializes.

The Boneh–Shoup companion at `learn/boneh-shoup/` follows version 0.6 of the free book, with 23 chapter entries and explicit boundaries for absent or unfinished text. The Understanding Cryptography companion at `learn/understanding-cryptography/` follows Paar, Pelzl and Güneysu's 2024 second edition: 14 numbered chapters plus a separately identified SHA-3 focus at §11.5. Both use the `guide` mechanism and direct menu links.

Understanding Cryptography resolves its repository slugs, lab titles and live URLs directly against `CATALOG.md`. Its complete static page provides original predict–experiment–explain activities and a coverage boundary for every study stop. Run `node learn/understanding-cryptography/build.mjs` to regenerate it, or add `--check` to detect drift. The storage key `crypto-lab-understanding-cryptography-2e-progress-v1` keeps its optional progress separate from the other companions; Chapter 11 and its SHA-3 focus have distinct identifiers.

After changing a hub path, regenerate `README.md` with `node tools/readme-sync.js`, then the knowledge-map catalog with `node tools/knowledge-map-sync.js` after the last `index.html` edit. The map records exact source hashes, so navigation changes require regeneration even when the exhibit cards are unchanged. The book companions are navigation/learning resources, so they do not change the standalone exhibit count.

The timestamping and fair-exchange workshops are hub-owned learning pages under `learn/`, not separate repositories or standalone catalog cards. Their number is not added to the standalone exhibit count. They are discoverable through the path and its chapter links.
